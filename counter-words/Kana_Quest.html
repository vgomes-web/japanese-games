Find the question word. It tells you which pattern to use in your answer.

りんごは いくつ ありますか？ (Ringo wa ikutsu arimasu ka?)
How many apples are there?

りんごが みっつ あります。 (Ringo ga mittsu arimasu.)
There are three apples.

    How do I answer a question about time?
    • なんじですか？ (Nanji desu ka?) asks what time it is. For a whole hour, say the hour with じ (ji).
    • Watch the readings for 4, 7, and 9. Say よじ・しちじ・くじ (yoji, shichiji, kuji).
    • For half past, add はん (han) after the hour. さんじはん (sanji han) means 3:30.
